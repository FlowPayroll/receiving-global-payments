## Common options for cross-border payments

| Method | Typical considerations | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **International bank transfer (SWIFT)** | Familiar bank-to-bank process; delivery can take several business days | Sending, receiving, intermediary, and conversion charges |
| **Wise / Payoneer** | Digital services with country- and account-dependent features | Eligibility, receiving details, withdrawal options, and total fees |
| **USDT transfers (TRC20 / ERC20 / BEP20)** | Blockchain settlement may be fast, but funds still require a suitable cash-out route | Network compatibility, token support, exchange access, price and issuer risks |
| **PayPal and similar services** | Convenient for clients already using those platforms | Merchant charges, holds, currency conversion, and withdrawal eligibility |
| **Exchange or P2P crypto conversion** | An additional route from digital assets to local currency | Counterparty exposure, escrow, fraud prevention, and local restrictions |

Availability, timing, and pricing vary by provider and jurisdiction.

## Where USDT may fit

USDT is a US-dollar-referenced stablecoin that some contractors use for international settlement. A transfer can arrive on-chain relatively quickly, but **USDT is not the same as an insured bank deposit or guaranteed dollar balance**. Redemption, market price, platform access, and local cash-out options involve risks.

Before transferring, confirm the exact supported token and blockchain with the recipient, verify the complete wallet address, and consider a small test transfer. Never assume funds sent on the wrong network can be recovered.

## Choosing an approach

1. **Both parties have access to SEPA?** Compare eligible euro bank-transfer options.
2. **Prefer a conventional payment account?** Review Wise, Payoneer, and local banking services where available.
3. **Considering stablecoins?** Check legal requirements, wallet support, network fees, cash-out options, and risks first.
4. **Need a widely recognized checkout option?** Compare PayPal or card payments against their effective fees.
5. **Want to manage collection and withdrawals in one workflow?** Explore [FlowPayroll](https://flowpayroll.net) and confirm the services and payment destinations supported for your account.
