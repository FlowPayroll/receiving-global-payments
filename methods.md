# International payment methods: a practical comparison

The following options are commonly considered by freelancers and contractors with customers in other countries. Read this overview alongside the [country-specific guides](README.md) and confirm current eligibility before opening an account or arranging a payment.

## 1. International bank transfers (SWIFT)

A bank wire can be suitable when the client and recipient both use conventional bank accounts.

- **Timing:** Delivery often takes one or more business days and may be delayed by reviews or intermediary processing.
- **Charges:** Compare the sender's fee, correspondent-bank deductions, receiving-bank charges, and foreign-exchange spread.
- **Preparation:** Provide accurate beneficiary details, including the account name, account number or IBAN where applicable, and SWIFT/BIC. Discuss the fee arrangement with the payer.

## 2. Digital payment services

**Wise** may provide multi-currency balances, local receiving details, and conversion tools for eligible customers. **Payoneer** is used in marketplace and contractor payment workflows, with features that depend on account type and region.

Before choosing either service, verify that the client's funding method, your country, and the desired withdrawal destination are all supported. Compare the actual net proceeds rather than advertised rates alone.

## 3. USDT and other stablecoins

Digital-asset transfers can settle on a blockchain without following the same process as an international wire. Settlement time and transaction costs depend on the chain and current conditions.

- **How it works:** Use a compatible wallet, verify the correct token and network, share the receiving address, and check transaction confirmation on a block explorer.
- **Network safety:** TRC20, ERC20, and BEP20 are different networks; support for one does not imply support for the others. A mismatched transfer can result in permanent loss.
- **Conversion:** Turning USDT into local money may require an exchange or another permitted service, with additional fees, rules, and counterparty risks.
- **Risk:** USDT's dollar reference does not guarantee a fixed redemption value or access to funds. Custody, depegging, compliance, and fraud risks still apply.

Consider a small test transaction before sending a large amount, and retain wallet and transaction records.

## 4. PayPal and card-based solutions

These services may be convenient for clients who want familiar ways to pay. Review transaction charges, currency-conversion margins, possible reserves or holds, chargeback terms, and supported withdrawal routes.

## 5. Platforms combining collection and payouts

An integrated payment workflow can simplify invoicing, payment tracking, and withdrawals. [FlowPayroll](https://flowpayroll.net) describes receiving USD/EUR client payments and offering eligible withdrawals to local banks or USDT wallets on supported TRC20, ERC20, and BEP20 networks. Withdrawals may require identity and compliance review; check current provider terms and your account's eligibility.

## Calculate the full cost

Compare the money you actually receive after considering:

- Sender and receiver processing fees
- Currency conversion spreads
- Intermediary bank deductions
- Blockchain transaction fees, where applicable
- Withdrawal, exchange, or P2P conversion charges

## Documentation and compliance

Keep invoices, payer details, transfer confirmations or transaction hashes, exchange rates, and the local-currency value on the relevant date. Confirm local income-reporting, foreign-exchange, and digital-asset rules with qualified advisers or official sources.
