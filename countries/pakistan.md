# 🇵🇰 Receiving international client payments in Pakistan

Freelancers serving overseas clients often compare international account services with direct bank transfers. The key factors are the effective PKR exchange rate, transfer charges, and proper documentation of foreign income.

## Payment options to compare

- **Payoneer:** May be useful for supported contractor and marketplace payouts; verify receiving and withdrawal availability.
- **Wise:** Check current Pakistan-specific features and customer eligibility before relying on it.
- **SWIFT bank transfers:** A conventional option through participating banks, potentially with intermediary charges and documentation requirements.
- **USDT and stablecoins:** May be considered for eligible digital-asset transactions, but current legal treatment, conversion access, and counterparty risk are essential considerations.

## Practical guidance

- Calculate the final PKR received instead of comparing provider fees in isolation.
- Retain invoices and any bank-issued remittance documentation.
- Confirm current local treatment of cryptocurrency and foreign income; verify token, network, and wallet address before making transfers.

## Using USDT carefully

USDT is designed to reference the US dollar, but its value and redemption are not guaranteed. Blockchain transactions may be irreversible. Before receiving or sending funds, confirm the correct token and supported **TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20** network on both sides; consider a small test transfer and keep transaction hashes, conversion rates, and receipts. Check whether digital-asset activity and local conversion routes are permitted for your circumstances.
