# 🇵🇭 Receiving international client payments in the Philippines

The Philippines supports a large community of remote contractors, freelancers, and virtual assistants serving overseas customers. Common priorities include convenient USD or EUR payments and predictable conversion into Philippine pesos.

## Payment options to compare

- **Wise and Payoneer:** Frequently considered for eligible cross-border contractor payments, with country-dependent account features.
- **SWIFT bank transfers:** A conventional receiving option, though charges may make smaller payments comparatively expensive.
- **USDT and stablecoins:** Digital-asset payments are an option some users explore; verify exchange access, local rules, token support, and related risks.
- **PayPal:** Familiar to many international clients, but fees, FX spreads, and withdrawal limits warrant comparison.

## Practical guidance

- Compare effective PHP conversion rates and the final amount credited.
- When appropriate, consolidate smaller transfers to reduce fixed charges while considering cash-flow needs.
- Save transaction confirmations and check local reporting requirements, especially if exchanging digital assets.

## Using USDT carefully

USDT is designed to reference the US dollar, but its value and redemption are not guaranteed. Blockchain transactions may be irreversible. Before receiving or sending funds, confirm the correct token and supported **TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20** network on both sides; consider a small test transfer and keep transaction hashes, conversion rates, and receipts. Check whether digital-asset activity and local conversion routes are permitted for your circumstances.
