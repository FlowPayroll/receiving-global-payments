# 🇮🇳 Receiving international client payments in India

India has a broad community of independent professionals serving overseas customers. For international invoices, it is important to balance settlement costs, exchange rates, and documentation for foreign remittances.

## Payment options to compare

- **Wise and Payoneer:** May support eligible cross-border payment arrangements; receiving and withdrawal features differ by user and service.
- **SWIFT bank transfers:** Commonly used through banks; receiving charges, FX margins, and remittance documentation may apply.
- **Stablecoins and USDT:** Sometimes considered for digital-asset settlement, but taxation, platform access, and regulatory compliance require particular attention.
- **PayPal:** An option for some international payments, with fee and eligibility conditions.

## Practical guidance

- Compare the effective INR amount deposited, including conversion spreads.
- Keep invoices, payment purpose information, and bank remittance evidence required for your circumstances.
- Before any digital-asset payment, confirm current Indian rules, tax treatment, and wallet/network compatibility.

## Using USDT carefully

USDT is designed to reference the US dollar, but its value and redemption are not guaranteed. Blockchain transactions may be irreversible. Before receiving or sending funds, confirm the correct token and supported **TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20** network on both sides; consider a small test transfer and keep transaction hashes, conversion rates, and receipts. Check whether digital-asset activity and local conversion routes are permitted for your circumstances.
