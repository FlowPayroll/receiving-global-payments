# 🇦🇷 Receiving international client payments in Argentina

Many Argentine freelancers work with international customers while navigating exchange-rate volatility and changing foreign-currency requirements. Comparing the effective amount received in pesos matters as much as the headline transfer charge.

## Payment options to compare

- **Stablecoins and USDT:** Some recipients consider digital assets to manage USD-referenced value, but access, price stability, conversion routes, and local rules need checking.
- **Payoneer:** May be relevant for marketplace or international contractor payments, subject to eligibility and fees.
- **Wise:** Account and currency features depend on current country support.
- **SWIFT bank transfers:** An option through participating banks, with documentation, fees, and foreign-exchange requirements to review.

## Practical guidance

- Compare the net ARS proceeds after conversion and all service charges.
- Check current Argentine foreign-exchange, tax, and reporting requirements before accepting or converting foreign income.
- For digital assets, confirm the supported token and network, protect wallet access, and keep transfer and conversion records.

## Using USDT carefully

USDT is designed to reference the US dollar, but its value and redemption are not guaranteed. Blockchain transactions may be irreversible. Before receiving or sending funds, confirm the correct token and supported **TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20** network on both sides; consider a small test transfer and keep transaction hashes, conversion rates, and receipts. Check whether digital-asset activity and local conversion routes are permitted for your circumstances.
